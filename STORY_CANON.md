# Soules story canon

## The old world and the first war

The Riftlands separated the main world from the Dreamscape. Monsters were much rarer and were not hostile. Each region had its own elemental civilisation.

The king of the Dreamscape entered the Sacred Temple in the Riftlands and corrupted core energy. The Dreamscape merged into the main world. Monsters rose, and the king himself became a monster and their king.

The elemental civilisations fought the invasion. They were destroyed one after another until only the Ignar Clan, Glacien Tribe, Voltis Enclave and Sylvar Circle remained.

Warriors from those four civilisations entered the temple. They stabilised the power with their lives and banished the Dreamscape King. The surviving civilisations concealed themselves behind wards and preserved different traditions and accounts of the sacrifice.

## Chapter One: the returning Dreamscape

The rift opens again and the Dreamscape falls back into the world. The corrupted king returns; his monsters are taking over the regions. The player begins in Whispering Woods with a Rusty Sword, learns the old history, frees the regions and defeats the king in the Dreamscape.

The existing boss name, The Dreaming God, is retained as an alias for the corrupted Dreamscape King. The Sacred Temple is the location already called Source Temple in the original game data; these are the same place, not two temples.

The reason the rift reopened is not revealed in Chapter One.

## Season Two: The Broken Bridge

After the king falls, a new villain tampers with the Core Stabiliser to open a bridge to the Creator's Realm. The attempt breaks it and unbalances Source energy. The player investigates the surviving villages' channels, stops the activation and repairs the stabiliser.

The current former-keeper villain name is Vael. The names of this new villain and the four ancestral warband leaders are additions that can be renamed without changing the user's history.

Repairing the present damage does not erase or replace the ancient warriors' sacrifice. Their original seal and the newly damaged mechanisms are related parts of the temple's long history.

## Season Three: the overarching antagonist

Season Three reveals that the same overarching antagonist behind the wider story caused the rift to reopen and the Dreamscape to return. Their identity, motive, connection to the ancient king and relationship with the Season Two villain remain open for development. Do not reveal this cause during Chapter One or replace it with an unrelated accidental reopening.

The Creator is not a required boss for either previous ending.

## Added clan memories

These are expansions around the established sacrifice, not replacements for it:

- Ilyra of Ignar led a warband that held the burning breach open.
- Tovan of Glacien led the effort to slow the corrupted current.
- Kael of Voltis helped bind the currents into a stabilising circuit.
- Miren of Sylvar anchored the seal to the living world.

All four groups paid with their lives. Each surviving civilisation remembers its part, giving its libraries, crafts and hidden location a reason to exist beyond loot.
