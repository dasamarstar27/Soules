# 100 Soules addons

Implemented catalogue as of 18 October 2026. Open World & Social → Adventures, projects & research. The entries below preserve the design names; the implementation checklist links each group to its code and tests. Related activities share reusable rules and quest stages. The existing chapter endings, rare hidden villages and permanent weapon runes are preserved.

## Quests and people

1. **The Unsent Letter:** deliver a fallen warrior's letter to a surviving relative; unlock family lore.
2. **Elian's Walking Staff:** repair the mentor's old staff through a short exploration quest.
3. **The Empty Chair:** help a village commemorate people lost during the second invasion.
4. **A Monster That Remembers:** investigate a creature retaining memories of its peaceful life.
5. **The Lost Caravan:** trace survivors across three regions and choose their safest return route.
6. **Children of the Old City:** escort refugees back to an abandoned settlement after clearing its threats.
7. **A Cook's Last Recipe:** recover a family cookbook and unlock a regional dish.
8. **The Bell Beneath the Lake:** follow a submerged bell's sound to a ruined civilisation.
9. **The False Keeper:** expose someone selling fake protective blessings.
10. **The Guardian's Debt:** help a defeated guardian protect its village without stealing its core.

## Ancient history

11. **Eleven Memorials:** recover a remembrance for each civilisation whose core was destroyed.
12. **Warband Echoes:** experience short playable memories of the warriors' final journey.
13. **The Fifth Witness:** discover an unreliable ancient account and compare its claims with the clans'.
14. **The Broken Standard:** restore a lost elemental civilisation's banner.
15. **Languages of the Fallen:** translate inscriptions through collected vocabulary.
16. **Temple Reconstruction Archive:** assemble diagrams showing how the ancient stabiliser worked.
17. **The King's Human Portrait:** find evidence of the Dreamscape King before his transformation.
18. **The Last Peaceful Day:** reconstruct life before the first merging through household relics.
19. **Names on the Wall:** identify unnamed warriors and restore their memorial inscriptions.
20. **The Survivor's Song:** collect verses preserved differently by the four clans.

## Village projects

21. **Village Storehouse:** fund shared storage for village construction materials.
22. **Road Repair Projects:** rebuild damaged routes to unlock local transport benefits.
23. **Community Greenhouse:** grow rare regional plants in a limited number of plots.
24. **Village Watch Training:** improve local defence through equipment donations and training quests.
25. **Public Kitchen:** turn donated food into meals for refugees and reputation rewards.
26. **Travelling Artisan Visits:** invite a temporary specialist through a village project.

28. **Public Noticeboard Projects:** let residents propose and vote on the next building project.

30. **Ruined Village Restoration:** rebuild one destroyed settlement without recreating its lost core.

## Crafting and professions

31. **Smithing Specialties:** choose between weapon tempering, balanced blades and heavy arms.
32. **Weaving Specialties:** develop armour focused on protection, movement or elemental resistance.
33. **Artisan Commissions:** fulfil equipment orders with clear stat and material requirements.
34. **Material Provenance:** crafted items remember the region where their materials were gathered.
35. **Repairable Crafting Tools:** maintain tools to improve crafting efficiency.
36. **Apprentice Assistance:** train an NPC assistant to help with limited batch crafting.
37. **Masterwork Trials:** prove a profession's mastery through a designed challenge rather than repetition alone.
38. **Reforging With Constraints:** alter an unbound weapon's physical stats while keeping bound runes permanent.
39. **Textile Patterns:** discover armour patterns through ruins, merchants and NPCs.
40. **Artisan Signatures:** let the player name and sign completed masterwork equipment.

## Exploration

41. **Fishing:** catch region-specific fish using bait and finite fishing spots.
42. **Mining Veins:** discover small ore deposits with limited extraction attempts.
43. **Weather Trails:** reveal different routes during storms, fog or clear weather.
44. **Tracking:** follow footprints and environmental clues instead of relying only on encounter rolls.
