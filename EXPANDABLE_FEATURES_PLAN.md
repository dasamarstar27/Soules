# Five expandable foundations

Implemented features are described below; the proposed updates are not implemented.

| Foundation available now | First expansion | Later expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story journal with chapter objectives, persistent evidence and a playable Season Two investigation | Optional objectives with consequences recorded in the journal | Branching outcomes that carry into Season Three |
| Village keeper conversations with remembered topics and a recorded promise to help | Named NPCs with their own short dialogue trees and requests | Relationships, village disputes and alternative quest resolutions |
| Six crafting professions with five ranks; real crafting/paid upgrades earn XP | Profession-specific recipes and clear next-rank rewards | Choose a specialty, with distinct recipes rather than unlimited stat stacking |
| Recorded village rumours that give directions without revealing locations | Rumour reliability, competing reports and a map notebook | Travelling informants, treasure leads and discoveries affected by reputation |
| Postgame Source anomalies with limited collectible fragments | Region-specific anomaly choices, costs and fragment recipes | Multi-stage anomaly encounters and community restoration projects |

Guardrails: preserve save compatibility, keep hidden villages rare, retain permanent weapon runes, and keep consumable supplies finite. Opening menus must never grant profession XP or refill stocks.

Chapter One stays the Dreamscape monster invasion. Monsters were once much rarer and peaceful; the Dreamscape boss is now sending hostile monsters to take over the world. Core Stabiliser sabotage belongs to Season Two after the original victory.
