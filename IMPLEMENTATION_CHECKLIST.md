## Scope and storage

- Trading, guilds and arena ghosts share one hosted instance's SQLite file. Separate offline computers do not share data, and arena ghosts are asynchronous rather than live PvP.
- Save slots, timeline, interface preferences and local run chronicles live in the browser. Export JSON backups for another browser/device. Community database data is not embedded in character exports.
- The Season Three antagonist's goal is revealed in that chapter. Its underlying motive and personal identity remain concealed. The four surviving cores are never taken from their villages.
- Construction projects and related addon activities share mechanics. The two 100-entry catalogues are not 200 independent engines.
