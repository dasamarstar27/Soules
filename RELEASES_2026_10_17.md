# Soules release history — 17 October 2026

Dates refer to the creator's Asia/Kuala_Lumpur timezone. These are the major published development batches, not 200 individually completed gameplay systems.

1. **Browser foundation** (`1b1ec6a`): playable browser interface, rune/effect engine, alchemy, save slots and village crafting. The initial simplified edition remains at `/edition`.
2. **Original text adventure and elemental villages** (`69959b2`): restored the original game behind the browser, grouped actions, live sidebar, region/structure colours, finite runes, permanent weapon bindings and the four clans' postgame services.
3. **Wandering and death corrections** (`8965774`): reliable encounters, full category access and character death ending the active run.
4. **Menus and merchant clarity** (`096cbba`): numbered top-level categories, secondary menus and visible prices before buying.
5. **Inventory and convenience pack** (`652f04d`): attack/defence/element/value sorting, secondary sorting, protected duplicate sales, testing unlocks and twelve interface conveniences.
6. **Village identity and canonical story** (`6a001d3`): different local services and quality, finite food, farms, notes/tags/directory, Elian and the Dreamscape history tied to playable progression.
7. **Complete normal rune pairs and the rebuilding interlude** (`a953c6f`): all 8,778 normal pairs, pair synergies and real effect abilities, local construction upgrades, removal of automatic scrolling, 20 post-victory side quests and the five-month Season Two transition.
8. **Living villages, community construction and ancient finds** (`aba442b`): Old Village start, 32 additional villages, finite local metal/wood stock, quarries/timber yards/watch posts, random development every 10–15 visits, 100 named construction projects, protected ruins/tomes/fragments and restored Riftlands temple infusion.
9. **Companion care and the complete field guide** (`dfc6207`): developer-mode removal from any prompt, prominent story-area questions, comprehensive tutorial and all 47 action explanations, regional companion diets/hunger/feeding, named three-stage evolution, stronger final-form support, old-save migration and crystal special moves.

10. **Short-reply journal following** (`c828da3`): short responses automatically follow near the bottom, while long passages and reading earlier entries retain their position. Rendered height accounts for mobile wrapping, and Latest remains a manual jump.

11. **Menus and attacks follow too** (`34f58c9`): ordinary menus, village choices, maps, selling and attacks follow automatically near the bottom; only very long blocks pause following. Reading earlier entries remains protected.

12. **Regional trinkets and full-hunger recovery** (current release): themed loot stays in its native regions across monster drops, caves, arenas and dungeons. Starting a wander at 100 hunger restores up to 3 HP before hunger decreases. Cache material selection errors are corrected.

## Scope of the two 100-entry catalogues

- `VILLAGE_PROJECTS_100.md`: 100 implemented construction projects sharing local construction, reputation and service-quality mechanics.
- `ADDON_CATALOG_100.md`: 100 broader proposed addons. This is still a design backlog; companion care and village work do not constitute completion of that entire list.
- `SEASON_THREE_GUIDE.md`: proposed future campaign. Protected ancient finds prepare for it; its full story and campaign are not released.
