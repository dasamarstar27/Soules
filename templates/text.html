Game words, explained

HP · Health
How much injury your character can survive. At zero health, the journey ends.
Mana
Energy spent on magic and certain abilities.
ATK · Attack
A measure of your fighting strength. Your weapon adds to it.
DEF · Defence
Protection that reduces incoming damage. Armour helps.
XP · Experience
Progress toward your next character level.
Souls / Gold
The money you spend at shops and workshops.
Hunger
Your food supply meter. Eat before it reaches zero. Starting a wander at full hunger restores a little health.
Rune
A magical item. Casting a loose rune spends it once; binding one to a weapon is permanent.
Status effect
A temporary condition such as poison, healing or extra protection. The sidebar shows its duration.
Turn / Shift
A turn is a combat step. A shift is work progress; each wander advances pending orders by one shift.
Save slot
A separate saved character or checkpoint in this browser. Export a backup to move it to another device.

Hindi narration · हिंदी में सुनें

पहले हिंदी की आवाज़ चुनें। फिर “हिंदी में सुनें” दबाएँ। अंग्रेज़ी पाठ का हिंदी अनुवाद भी ज़रूरी है।

  1. In Settings / Customise, choose Hindi as the narration language. The game automatically picks an installed Hindi voice.
  2. Windows: open Settings → Time & language → Language & region → Add a language → Hindi. Install the speech / text-to-speech option where offered. In some Windows versions voices are under Accessibility → Narrator → Add voices. Reopen the browser afterwards.
  3. iPad: look in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content (or Read & Speak) → Voices → Hindi and download a voice. Browser support varies; Chrome or Safari may expose different voices.
  4. A supported browser can translate English into Hindi automatically, sometimes after downloading its translation model. If that is unavailable, use Chrome’s menu → Translate → Hindi, then select a translated Hindi passage and press Read latest aloud. Browser translation can require internet.
  5. Press Stop reading any time. Hindi mode never silently reads untranslated English as though it were Hindi. The written game and answer numbers remain playable as before.
