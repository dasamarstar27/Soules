Game words, explained

HP · Health
How much injury your character can survive. At zero health, the journey ends.
Mana
Energy spent on magic and certain abilities.
ATK · Attack
A measure of your fighting strength. Your weapon adds to it.
DEF · Defence
Protection that reduces incoming damage. Armour helps.
XP · Experience
Progress toward your next character level.
Souls / Gold
The money you spend at shops and workshops.
Hunger
Your food supply meter. Eat before it reaches zero. Starting a wander at full hunger restores a little health.
Rune
A magical item. Casting a loose rune spends it once; binding one to a weapon is permanent.
Status effect
A temporary condition such as poison, healing or extra protection. The sidebar shows its duration.
Turn / Shift
A turn is a combat step. A shift is work progress; each wander advances pending orders by one shift.
Save slot
A separate saved character or checkpoint in this browser. Export a backup to move it to another device.
