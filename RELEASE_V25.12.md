# Soules V25.12

Village option 17 is now Build / upgrade village facilities. Missing ordinary services can be constructed for 5 Timber, 4 Stone, 2 Iron fittings and 100 souls. New buildings start at Level 1; existing and constructed buildings can be upgraded to Level 5 using the displayed costs. Confirmations and material-conversion choices remain available through tablet buttons.

Constructed services immediately join the village menu, live sidebar, crafting hub, worker choices and compatible community projects. They persist per character and village through travel, directory revisits and save import/export. Old saves need no conversion. Removing beta-mode state also removes its constructed-service availability when the village refreshes. Different villages retain their native quality and starting facilities. Source/Imbuing Temples remain exclusive to elemental villages.

Farm levels increase effective quality and therefore regional ingredient yield. A newly built farm yields two ingredients; the next level yields three before other bonuses. Upgrades do not reset a harvested farm’s 20-wander regrowth cycle. Restaurant capacity updates on its next normal restock. Village natural development can also improve the new facilities. Simple gameplay is unchanged.

Validation: 269 unit tests passed, including construction costs, missing-service availability, all ordinary facility types, level cap, farm growth/regrowth, no spending on failed/cancelled requests, Budget Run, save restoration, village locality and record removal. Browser checks passed for tablet build/confirm/upgrade, immediate sidebar/menu/workbench availability, saved directory revisit, existing village upgrades, farm limits and version downloads.
