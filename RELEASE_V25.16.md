# Soules V25.16

Village → Shops & crafting → Brew potions now offers B: Bulk brew and bottle. Choose two ingredients, a finite quantity (up to the pouch limit or 1,000 per batch), and confirm. Each potion consumes its own pair, rolls the existing alchemy/rank preservation chance and awards normal crafting/challenge progress. The batch bottles every result and prints one completion summary. Same-ingredient pairs require two copies per potion. Unknown combinations remain single-brew experiments; cancelled, invalid or unaffordable ingredient quantities spend nothing. Tablet buttons cover ingredients, quantity steppers and Yes/No. Single brewing remains available.

Sidebar updates now preserve existing DOM cards and controls instead of replacing the entire sidebar. Focused selections, expanded village panels and the visible reading anchor survive live updates, including content growing above the reader. Native scroll anchoring is disabled in that panel. Character stats come first, then an active enemy, local village and region; other panels remain available below. This fixes the causes reproduced in local browser checks, rather than introducing automatic sidebar following.

Whispering Woods’ original forest-green region palette was already present, but ordinary villages were overriding it with brown. Woods villages now keep that original green too. The warm brown palette is assigned to Ashen Wastes. Other structure/elemental colours and fixed/custom preferences retain their existing rules. Simple gameplay is unchanged.

Validation: 286 unit tests passed. Batch tests cover ingredient costs, duplicate ingredients, preservation, XP/challenges, potion save restoration, cancellation, recipe/bench requirements and quantity limits. Browser checks passed for actual tablet bulk brewing, quantity clamp, confirmation, bottled results and feedback; stable sidebar node identity, focus, open panels, scroll/read anchors and delayed polls; Woods village/Ashen/custom colours; existing equipment/potion actions, village farming/upgrades/notes, sidebar reading position and version downloads.
