# Season Two: After the Dream

Implemented in the original text edition through `season_two.py`.

Chapter 1 ends after unlocking the main regions and defeating The Dreaming God in The Dreamscape. The Creator is not required.

The four existing rare elemental villages remain in their original regions. Discovering one records it under Structures for return visits. Their expanded services open in Chapter 2:

- Element-themed blades, coats, staffs, food and potion stock. Shop quantities persist and are finite for the character.
- A named keeper in each village, dialogue and paid introductory training.
- Two tomes per village, available only in that village's library, teaching its offensive touch and healing art.
- One ingredient-delivery side quest per village, with a one-time reward.
- Access to the village crafting hub.

Combat option 9 uses a learned elemental art and spends mana. Knowledge persists in saves. Offensive arts deal modest damage and apply their element's status; healing arts restore capped HP.

Season Three is proposed separately in `SEASON_THREE_PLAN.md`.
