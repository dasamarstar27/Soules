# Chapter 2: planned for later

The elemental villages already exist in the original game (including Ignar Clan). Their data and original terminal mechanics are preserved. The current browser edition has not yet connected those discovery/visit encounters. This plan expands the existing villages; it does not introduce them as a new idea.

- Chapter 1 ends after unlocking all main regions and defeating The Dreaming God in The Dreamscape. The Creator is not required.
- Chapter 2 is postgame. Preserve the rare-discovery design of the existing elemental villages.
- The main menu needs a Structures directory for all discovered structures. Once discovered, a structure—including an elemental village—can be revisited without finding it again.
- Each elemental village needs its own village menu, beyond taking its core.
- Villages should offer element-themed equipment, goods and food, people to meet, and side quests.
- Elemental village libraries should offer new elemental tomes. These special tomes belong only in elemental villages.
- People and tomes can teach simple elemental manipulation.
- Immediate priority: let the creator see and play the existing game. Do not implement this future update yet.
