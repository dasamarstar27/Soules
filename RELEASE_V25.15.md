# Soules V25.15

Ordinary single-enemy fights show a planned intention in the text and enemy sidebar before the player chooses. Monsters may attack, wind up a heavy strike, pounce, guard, recover, siphon life or stumble. Spirit/mage-like and wolf/beast-like families have different weights, listed in the tutorial/dictionary. Existing intentions survive cancelled or invalid choices; stun overrides the plan. Guardians retain their scripted rules; multi-enemy tactical/raid encounters retain their existing specialized actions.

10 Guard is free and reduces the incoming attack by 65% before other modifiers. 11 Interrupt costs 3 mana and cancels a heavy strike or pounce; an unavailable or invalid interrupt spends no mana. Monster guard halves direct strikes. Recovery replaces an attack, heals 8% of maximum HP, is planned only below 60% HP and is capped at two uses per fight. A mistake skips the monster action. Life siphon is a weaker hit that heals a quarter of damage dealt. Easy/Normal scaling and existing evolution/reward rules remain in effect.

Villages now show six short categories: Food & rest, Shops & crafting, Build & manage, People & quests, Notes & bookmarks, Activities; 7 leaves from the root. Each category shows only available services. Actions return to the selected submenu; 0 returns to the village categories. Sidebar shortcuts remain usable at village/category prompts. All existing village actions, services, building progress and saves are preserved. Older regression helpers address legacy actions explicitly where their test is about that service rather than category navigation. Simple gameplay is unchanged.

Validation: 279 unit tests passed, including intent persistence, archetypes, limited recovery, stun priority, guard damage, paid interrupts and actual combat reactions, plus complete village-action coverage and navigation. Browser checks passed for tablet village categories/back/harvest/build, an actual tracked fight with Guard/Interrupt, shop confirmations, construction and saved revisit, village notes/farming, Easy reading, all four monster evolution stages in both difficulties, worker orders and player quality-of-life actions, original menus, world-map travel and version downloads.
