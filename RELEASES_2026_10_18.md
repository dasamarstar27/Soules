# Soules deferred-systems release — 18 October 2026

The original text edition now connects the complete 100-addon catalogue and previously deferred systems to working player menus. The earlier 100 construction projects remain separate. See IMPLEMENTATION_CHECKLIST.md for every name, implementation module and validation scope.

Major additions:

1. Village commissions, alchemists, gathering crews, apprentices and level/quality-based results. Supplies and fees are held up front; local work/rest advances orders; collection is one-time.
2. Village defence assignments, shared storage, roads, gardens, public kitchens, visiting artisans, festivals, building votes, wells and restored refuges.
3. Twenty authored side stories with travel, evidence, supplies, resolutions and post-victory rebuilding credit.
4. Fishing, finite mining, weather trails, tracking, maps, camps, caravan escorts, crossings and peaceful wildlife.
5. Targeted multi-enemy tactical combat, intentions, nonlethal practice, restrictions, hazards, interrupts, counterspells, mercy and returning spared characters.
6. Crafting specialties, material origins/mastery, tools, apprentices, trials, constrained reforging, patterns and signatures.
7. Rune research, tested-fusion notebook/provenance, distinct rune seals, practice, field magic, philosophy and muted-by-default effect sounds.
8. Cooking and Feasts, toxicity, herb drying, medical requests, recipe notes, rations, gardens, researched substitutions, clan banquet and bounded potion blending.
9. Companion quests, camp dialogue, temperaments, accessories, sanctuary, mentor memories, friendships, quest partners, reunions and keepsakes. Evolution now comes from actual help in victories, never feeding.
10. Save timeline, run chronicles, keyboard remapping, concise combat announcements, map notes, routes, build planning, character-sheet export and optional statistics.
11. Daily calendar rewards; working cosmetics, guilds, festivals, modes, adventure pass, rituals, arena, trading, town facilities, factions, treasure maps, mounts and New Game+. Shared features remain local to one hosted server.
12. Finished advanced skill purchases, daily/weekly event counters, live boss rush and records, equipment collection bonuses, legendary forging, usable prestige shops, artifacts and safe fresh prestige ascension.
13. Playable Season Three expedition with Sera, protected-fragment ward, archive revelation, paired crafting, confrontation, custody choices and optional Vault. The villain's personal motive is deliberately unrevealed; the male Creator retains his benevolent and cruel aspects.
14. Expanded tutorial, potion dictionary, bounded save validation, workshop sidebar and regression tests.

Validation: 184 automated tests plus seven browser smoke/regression scripts passed. Launchers remain included; no new gameplay dependency is required. There is no public game deployment in this release. Export important browser saves before moving to the new extracted folder and keep the same launcher address/browser profile.

The 17 October history records what was released then; its proposal-only notes are superseded by this release and the updated current guides.
